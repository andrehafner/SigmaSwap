SigmaStats

Ergo at a glance, in your wallet

Open it in your Ergo wallet

SigmaStats is a Mosaik dApp: it runs inside the Ergo wallet app.

  1. Copy the address below.
  2. In the Ergo wallet app open dApps (Mosaik) and paste it into the address field.
  3. Look at the network, open "Portfolio", or set a price alert.

Good to know

  • Read-only. SigmaStats never builds a transaction and never asks you to sign anything.
  • Network. Block height, block time, hashrate, difficulty and waiting transactions, from the public Ergo explorer.
  • Portfolio. What an address holds, valued in ERG at the ErgoDex pool prices. The address is looked up, not stored.
  • Price alerts. Pick ERG or a token and a price. The service checks once a minute and your wallet shows a notice when it is reached.
  • Prices. They are ErgoDex pool prices, not exchange quotes; the ERG price is its SigUSD pool price.
