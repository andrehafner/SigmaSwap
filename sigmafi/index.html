SigmaFi

Peer-to-peer loans on Ergo, in your wallet

Open it in your Ergo wallet

SigmaFi is a Mosaik dApp: it runs inside the Ergo wallet app.

  1. Copy the address below.
  2. In the Ergo wallet app open dApps (Mosaik) and paste it into the address field.
  3. Choose your wallet address, then lend, offer to lend, borrow, or open "My loans".

Good to know

  • Independent. This app is not made by the SigmaFi or Lithos developers. It uses the SigmaFi V2 bond contracts (the "ZC Standard") as they publish them.
  • Borrow. Lock tokens as collateral and ask for ERG. Repay in full before maturity and the collateral comes back; after maturity you can no longer repay and the lender takes all of it.
  • Lend. Fund someone's request, or post a standing offer that borrowers with SigUSD collateral can take. You receive a bond token for each loan: it is your claim, so keep it.
  • My loans. Cancel a request or an offer, repay, add collateral, move unpaid collateral to its vault, and collect a settled loan.
  • New contracts. SigmaFi V2 is new and its authors still describe it as not deployed. Use small amounts. Signing needs the 2.5 release of the Ergo wallet app.
