Open it in your Ergo wallet

SigmaMint is a Mosaik dApp: it runs inside the Ergo wallet app, where you fill in the details and sign.

  1. Copy the address below.
  2. In the Ergo wallet app open dApps (Mosaik) and paste it into the address field.
  3. Choose "Mint a token" or "Mint an NFT", fill in the form, preview, then mint.

Good to know

  • Cost. Only the Ergo network fee (about 0.001 ERG). No service fee.
  • Permanent. A mint cannot be undone or edited. Check the name and the numbers first.
  • Names are not unique. Anyone can mint a token with any name; a name does not make a token official.
  • Your keys stay yours. SigmaMint only prepares the transaction; your wallet shows it and signs it.
