SigmaLP

Provide liquidity to the ErgoDex pools

Open it in your Ergo wallet

SigmaLP is a Mosaik dApp: it runs inside the Ergo wallet app, where you choose a pool and sign.

  1. Copy the address below.
  2. In the Ergo wallet app open dApps (Mosaik) and paste it into the address field.
  3. Choose "Add liquidity" or "Your positions".

Good to know

  • What you get. LP tokens that stand for your share of the pool. Your share earns the pool's trading fees.
  • The risk. When the price moves, the pool rebalances. You get back a different mix of the two tokens than you put in, which can be worth less than simply holding them.
  • Direct. One transaction with the pool, signed in your wallet: it completes exactly as shown or not at all.
  • No service fee. You pay only the Ergo network fee.
