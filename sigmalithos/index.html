SigmaLithos

The Lithos mining pool, in your wallet

Open it in your Ergo wallet

SigmaLithos is a Mosaik dApp: it runs inside the Ergo wallet app.

  1. Copy the address below.
  2. In the Ergo wallet app open dApps (Mosaik) and paste it into the address field.
  3. Choose "Pool dashboard", "My mining" or "Trade LIT".

Good to know

  • Independent. This app is not made by the Lithos project. Lithos itself is at lithosprotocol.org.
  • Pool dashboard. Hashrate, blocks, what mining on Lithos pays, the collateral market and the LIT token, as Lithos publishes them.
  • My mining. What a mining address received from Lithos lately, read from the blockchain. It does not connect to, set up or control a miner.
  • Trade LIT. Buy or sell LIT on LithosDex with an order. A Lithos miner fills it at the pool price, never below your minimum, usually within an hour or so; until then you can cancel it. Trading needs Ergo wallet app version 2.5.1 or newer.
  • Small market. LIT is thinly traded. Large trades move the price a lot, and an order can wait a long time.
