SigmaLithos

The Lithos mining pool, in your wallet

Open it in your Ergo wallet

SigmaLithos is a Mosaik dApp: it runs inside the Ergo wallet app.

  1. Copy the address below.
  2. In the Ergo wallet app open dApps (Mosaik) and paste it into the address field.
  3. Choose "Pool dashboard", "My mining" or "Trade LIT".

Good to know

  • Independent. This app is not made by the Lithos project. Lithos itself is at lithosprotocol.org.
  • Pool dashboard. Hashrate, blocks, what mining on Lithos pays, the collateral market and the LIT token, as Lithos publishes them.
  • My mining. What a mining address received from Lithos lately, read from the blockchain. It does not connect to, set up or control a miner.
  • Trade LIT. Buy or sell LIT on LithosDex: swap directly with the pool in one transaction, or place an order. A Lithos miner fills an order at the pool price, never below your minimum, usually within a few minutes; until then you can cancel it. Signing needs the 2.5 release of the Ergo wallet app from Google Play or the Apple App Store, or the latest 2.5 Android APK from GitHub.
  • Provide liquidity. Add ERG and LIT to the LithosDex pool, collect your share of the trading fees, close the position. The position is an NFT in your wallet: do not send it away.
  • Lend collateral. Lock about 3 ERG and a LIT permit so miners are paid the moment a block is found, and earn the difference when that block's reward reaches you. It cannot be cancelled and can stay locked a long time. One position per address.
  • SigmaFi loans. Borrow ERG against tokens you hold, lend ERG to someone else for interest, or post a standing offer to lend, with SigmaFi V2 bonds. A loan not repaid in full by maturity costs the borrower all the collateral. New contracts: use small amounts.
  • Small market. LIT is thinly traded. Large trades move the price a lot, and an order can wait a long time.
