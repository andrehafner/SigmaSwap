SigmaSwap

Swap ERG and Ergo tokens on the ErgoDex liquidity pools

Open it in your Ergo wallet

SigmaSwap is a Mosaik dApp: it runs inside the Ergo wallet app, and every swap is reviewed and signed there. Nothing is installed and no account is needed.

  1. Copy the address below.
  2. In the Ergo wallet app open dApps (Mosaik) and paste it into the address field.
  3. Choose your wallet address, the tokens and the amount, preview, then swap.

How a swap works

  • Direct. One transaction between your wallet and the pool: it either completes exactly as previewed or does not happen at all.
  • Non-custodial. SigmaSwap only prepares the transaction. Your wallet shows it and signs it; your keys and funds never leave your wallet.
  • No service fee. You pay the pool's own fee and the Ergo network fee.
