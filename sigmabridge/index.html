SigmaBridge

Send ERG and tokens from Ergo to other chains

Open it in your Ergo wallet

SigmaBridge is a Mosaik dApp: it runs inside the Ergo wallet app, where you fill in the transfer and sign.

  1. Copy the address below.
  2. In the Ergo wallet app open dApps (Mosaik) and paste it into the address field.
  3. Choose what to send, the chain, and your address on that chain. Preview, check the address, then bridge.

Good to know

  • Out of Ergo only. To Cardano, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Bitcoin, Bitcoin Runes, Dogecoin and Firo, depending on the token. To bring funds to Ergo, start in the other chain's wallet at app.rosen.tech.
  • Cannot be reversed. If the address on the other chain is wrong, the funds are lost. Paste it and check it.
  • Bridge fees. Rosen charges a bridge fee and the other chain's network fee, taken from the amount. Small amounts are not worth sending; the app shows the minimum.
  • Swapuz, the second option. Sells your ERG for another coin (BTC, ETH, USDT, XMR and many more) and pays it to your address on that coin's chain. Swapuz is an exchange company: it holds the ERG until it pays out, the rate is an estimate, the minimum is several hundred ERG, and it can hold an exchange for identity checks.
  • Who does what. SigmaBridge prepares the transaction and your wallet signs it. The Rosen bridge, an independent project, delivers on the other chain, usually within an hour or two.
